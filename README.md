# CS F407 Artificial Intelligence: Lab Work

Solutions to the hands-on labs of CS F407 (AY 2026-27, Sem 1). Handouts are in the [course repository](https://github.com/tirtharajdash/CS-F407-AI-AY2026-27-S1).

| # | Lab | Handout | Folder | Main files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neural Models (XOR, backprop, activations, softmax) | `materials/neur_models_lab.pdf` | [Lab 1 Neural Models](Lab%201%20Neural%20Models) | `xor_lab.py`, `SUBMISSION.md` |
| 2 | Agents (goal-based warehouse navigation) | `materials/agents_lab.pdf` | [Lab 2 Agents](Lab%202%20Agents) | `warehouse_agent.py`, `SUBMISSION.md` |
| 3 | Search (A*, BFS, heuristics) | `materials/search_lab.pdf` | [Lab 3 Search](Lab%203%20Search) | `astar.py`, `SUBMISSION.md` |
| 4 | Logic (logical planning, Prolog verifier) | `materials/logic_lab.pdf` | [Lab 4 Logic](Lab%204%20Logic) | `planner.py`, `planner.pl`, `SUBMISSION.md` |
| 5 | Bayesian Networks with an LLM | `notebooks/llm_bn.ipynb` | [Lab 5 BN with LLM](Lab%205%20BN%20with%20LLM) | `llm_bn_completed.ipynb` |
| 6 | Learning AR models (Transformers, Ollama) | `labs/transformers/` | [Lab 6 Transformers](Lab%206%20Transformers) | `transformers_lab.ipynb` |
| 7 | Bayesian Networks and autoregressive LMs | `materials/BN_lab.pdf` | [Lab 7 Bayesian Networks](Lab%207%20Bayesian%20Networks) | `ngram_lm.py`, `SUBMISSION.md` |

Each folder has its own `README.md` with instructions for running it. Labs 1 to 4 and 7 are plain Python scripts (Lab 1 needs PyTorch). Labs 5 and 6 are Jupyter notebooks, saved with their outputs.
